Public procurement represents a significant share of economic activity worldwide. Governments and public agencies purchase goods, services and infrastructure across industries, creating valuable opportunities for suppliers.

Finding the right opportunities, however, can be challenging. Suppliers often deal with expensive subscriptions, delayed notifications, irrelevant information and limited flexibility.

Tenders & Bids was built to make global public procurement information more precise, timely and accessible. Discover relevant opportunities, evaluate them before committing, and access full tender information when you're ready.

Tender information is updated every 4 hours, helping suppliers stay closer to newly published opportunities while keeping control over what they choose to access.
